Before you get started.

Do I need an account?

You can create and edit projects without an account.

Which devices can I create videos for?

Create videos for iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.

What does the app check before export?

AppCut checks video duration and format so you can fix issues before export.

Can I export files to my Mac?

Export MP4 (H.264) videos and PNG frames. Pro adds ProRes 422 HQ (MOV) and watermark-free export.

What do I need to upload directly?

You need Pro and App Store Connect API credentials. Add your credentials in AppCut.

Where are my projects and credentials stored?

Projects are files on your Mac. AppCut stores App Store Connect API credentials in macOS Keychain.

How do I make my first video?

Follow the in-app onboarding or the getting-started guide from import to export.
