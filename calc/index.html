Mortgage calculator FAQ

Does the mortgage calculator include taxes, insurance and PMI?

Yes. Add each cost to estimate the full monthly payment rather than principal and interest alone.

Learn more

How much house can I afford based on income?

Use the affordability calculator with income, monthly debt and a comfortable housing budget. Treat the result as a planning range, not lender approval.

Learn more

How much interest can an extra mortgage payment save?

The answer depends on the amount and timing. Bricks Calc compares the new payoff path with your original schedule.

Learn more

What does an amortization schedule show?

It shows the principal, interest and remaining balance for every payment over the loan term.

Learn more

When does refinancing start to make sense?

Compare the new monthly savings with closing costs, break-even time and the total cost over the period you expect to keep the loan.

Learn more

Is a 15-year or 30-year mortgage better?

A 15-year term usually costs more each month and less in total interest. A 30-year term can preserve more monthly flexibility.

Learn more

Can I include rental income in the mortgage calculation?

Yes. Add expected rent and property expenses to see an estimated monthly carrying cost. Use conservative inputs.

Learn more

Does Bricks Calc require an account or internet connection?

No account is required. The core calculator works locally, and saved plans can sync with iCloud when enabled.
