Frequently asked questions

Is the beta open?

Yes. Request access using the form on this page. Requests are reviewed manually, and approved testers receive a TestFlight invitation by email.

Will APEs remain free after the beta?

APEs is free during the beta. Pricing for the full release has not been announced.

What about early adopters?

Early beta testers will receive something special when the app launches.

What is APEs?

APEs stands for App Preview Editor Studio. It is an App Store creative studio for Mac, built to help you create, update, check, and deliver the assets that show your app at its best.

Who is it for?

Indie Apple developers and small app teams who create and update their own App Store assets.

What can I create with APEs?

Create App Preview videos, Search Results Videos, Product Page Headers, and visuals for In-App Event Cards and Event Details Pages. Available App Store placements depend on your app and Apple’s support for each format.

Which Apple platforms are supported?

APEs runs on Mac. App Preview presets cover iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, including portrait and landscape where App Store Connect supports them.

Can I create previews for multiple languages?

Yes. Keep separate device and language timelines in the same project. You adapt the text and media for each language; APEs does not translate them automatically.

Can I update a preview after my app changes?

Yes. Replace or relink screenshots and recordings while preserving compatible timing, text, and styling.

Does APEs shorten my video to meet a duration limit?

No. APEs checks whether your video exceeds Apple’s applicable duration limit. You decide where to trim it. Auto Cut can trim pauses and dead time, but it does not automatically fit your video to an App Store duration limit.

Can I upload directly to App Store Connect?

Yes. Add your App Store Connect API credentials and configure your app to upload previews, screenshots, search videos, and header videos to supported destinations. Uploading does not publish an asset or guarantee approval.

How does APEs protect my privacy?

App Store Connect credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Projects and media are stored locally. Files you choose to upload are sent to App Store Connect.

How do I build my first App Preview?

Import your screenshots and recordings, arrange your best moments, then refine timing, text, audio, and motion. Review the checks, make any edits you need, and export or upload.

What can I export?

H.264 or ProRes 422 HQ video, plus individual PNG frames. Choose the format that fits your destination.

Do App Store Checks guarantee approval?

No. APEs flags common technical issues, duration overruns, and potentially risky text such as pricing or “#1” claims for your review. Apple makes the final decision on App Store acceptance.
