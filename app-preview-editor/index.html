Before you get started.

Do I need a separate account?

No. You don’t need a separate APEs account to create and edit a project. Direct upload uses your own App Store Connect API credentials.

Which devices and languages can I prepare?

APEs runs on Mac. App Preview presets cover iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, with portrait and landscape where supported. Keep separate language timelines and adapt the text and media yourself; APEs does not translate them automatically.

Does APEs shorten my video to meet a duration limit?

No. APEs checks whether your video exceeds Apple’s applicable duration limit. You choose where to trim. Auto Cut can trim pauses and dead time, but it does not automatically fit a video to an App Store duration limit.

Can I save files locally?

Yes. Save videos as H.264 MP4 or ProRes 422 HQ MOV files, and export individual frames as PNG images.

What do I need for direct upload?

Your App Store Connect API credentials, the right permissions for your app, and a supported destination. Some uploads require an editable app version. In-App Event videos are exported to a folder for you to add in App Store Connect.

Do App Store checks guarantee approval?

No. APEs flags common technical issues and potentially risky text for your review. Apple makes the final acceptance decision. Uploading does not publish an asset.

Where are my projects and credentials stored?

Projects are saved as files on your Mac, and App Store Connect API credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Files you choose to upload are sent to App Store Connect.
