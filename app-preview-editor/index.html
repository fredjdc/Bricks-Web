Before you get started.

Do I need an account?

No app account is required to create and edit projects.

Which devices can I create app previews for?

App Preview Editor runs on Mac. Create app previews for iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, and keep separate device and language timelines in one project.

What does the app check before export?

It checks video duration and format. Review the results and make any needed changes before exporting.

Can I export files to my Mac?

Yes. Export videos as MP4 (H.264) or individual frames as PNG images. Pro also includes ProRes 422 HQ MOV exports.

What do I need to upload directly?

Connect your App Store Connect account using your API credentials to upload supported videos from the editor.

Where are my projects and credentials stored?

Projects are saved as files on your Mac. Your App Store Connect API credentials are stored in macOS Keychain.

How do I make my first video?

Start with the in-app onboarding, or follow the getting-started guide for an import-to-export walkthrough.
