Frequently asked questions

Is the beta open?

Yes. Request access using the form on this page. Requests are reviewed manually, and approved testers receive a TestFlight invitation by email.

Will App Preview Editor remain free after the beta?

App Preview Editor is free during the beta. Pricing for the full release has not been announced.

What about early adopters?

Early beta testers will receive something special when the app launches.

Is App Preview Editor a full video editor?

No. It is built specifically for creating, updating, checking, and delivering App Preview videos.

Who is it for?

Apple developers, product designers, small app teams, marketers, and ASO specialists who need a focused App Preview workflow on Mac.

Which Apple platforms are supported?

App Preview Editor supports iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, including portrait and landscape presets where App Store Connect supports them.

Can I create App Preview Variants for multiple languages?

Yes. Keep separate device and language timelines together in the same project.

Can I update a preview after my app changes?

Yes. Replace or relink screenshots and recordings while preserving compatible timing and styling.

Can I upload directly to App Store Connect?

Yes. After adding App Store Connect API credentials and configuring an app target, you can add or replace preview slots from the export workflow.

How does App Preview Editor protect my privacy?

App Store Connect credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Projects and media are stored locally. Uploading sends your exported preview to App Store Connect.

How do I build my first App Preview?

Import your media, arrange clips on the timeline, then refine timing, text, audio, and motion. You stay in control of each edit.

What can I export?

H.264 or ProRes 422 HQ video, plus a PNG poster frame.

Does App Preview Editor guarantee App Store approval?

No. App Preview Editor checks common technical and project issues. Apple makes the final decision on App Store acceptance.
