Before you get started.

Do I need a separate account?

You can create and edit projects without a separate APEs account.

Which devices and languages can I prepare?

APEs runs on Mac. Prepare App Previews for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, and keep your language versions together in one project.

What does APEs check before export?

APEs checks video duration and format. Review the results, then trim or adjust your video on the timeline.

Can I save files locally?

Yes. Save videos as H.264 MP4 or ProRes 422 HQ MOV files, and export individual frames as PNG images.

What do I need for direct upload?

Direct upload connects to your App Store Connect account using your API credentials.

Where are my projects and credentials stored?

Projects are saved as files on your Mac. Your App Store Connect API credentials are stored in macOS Keychain.
