Before you get started.

Do I need an account?

No app account is required to create and edit projects. Direct uploads require your App Store Connect API credentials and Pro.

Which devices can I create videos for?

App Preview Editor runs on Mac. Create videos for iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, with separate device and language timelines in one project.

What does the app check before export?

It checks video duration and format. Review the results and make any needed changes before exporting.

Can I export files to my Mac?

Yes. Export videos as MP4 (H.264) or individual frames as PNG images. Pro also includes ProRes 422 HQ MOV exports and watermark-free exports.

What do I need to upload directly?

You need Pro and App Store Connect API credentials. Connect your account in the app to upload supported videos from the editor.

Where are my projects and credentials stored?

Projects are saved as files on your Mac. Your App Store Connect API credentials are stored in macOS Keychain.

How do I make my first video?

Follow the in-app onboarding or the getting-started guide for a walkthrough from importing your media to exporting your video.
