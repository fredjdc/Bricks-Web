# Bricks Leads — Content Strategy

> This document is the single source of truth for all social media content for Bricks Leads.
> It is used by both the human team and the Content Pipeline Agent.
> Update it when product positioning, audience understanding, platform strategy, or launch phase changes.

---

## The App

**Bricks Leads** is a private, offline-first CRM for solo real estate agents in Peru. It runs natively on iPhone. All data stays on the device. Apple Intelligence handles contact extraction and message drafting locally — nothing is processed remotely.

It captures leads from WhatsApp and email, organizes contacts and listings, matches buyers to properties, and helps draft follow-up messages and portal copy — all without internet.

**Current status:** Pre-launch. App Store listing pending.
**Platforms:** iPhone (primary). iPad and Mac on roadmap.
**Monetization:** Freemium — free tier with limits, paid upgrade available.
**Language:** Spanish only. This is a Peru-specific product for a Spanish-speaking audience.

For the full product overview, see [overview.md](./overview.md).

## Conversion Funnel

Content serves a specific goal at each stage. Every post should map to one stage.

| Stage | Goal | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Realtor sees the problem named and feels understood | Reach, impressions |
| **Interest** | Realtor sees the product and recognizes it as a solution | Profile visits, link clicks |
| **Consideration** | Realtor visits `leads.bricks.pe` and considers signing up | Landing page sessions |
| **Capture** | Realtor joins the waitlist | Email signups |
| **Activation** | After launch: first contact or listing created | In-app event |
| **Upgrade** | Realtor hits free tier limit and upgrades | StoreKit purchase |

Pre-launch content focuses on Awareness → Capture (waitlist).
Launch-week content focuses on Capture → Activation (download + first use).

## Who We're Talking To

### Primary Audience

**Solo real estate agents in Peru working from their iPhone.**

They manage 10–30 active leads at any given time. Most of their lead flow comes through WhatsApp — phone numbers, voice notes, portal inquiries forwarded from email. They don't have an assistant. They don't use a CRM. Their "system" is a combination of WhatsApp chats, saved contacts, and memory.

The problem they feel most acutely: a lead comes in on WhatsApp, they respond, intend to follow up, and forget. The lead goes cold. This happens weekly.

They are not looking for enterprise software. They want something that works on their phone, doesn't require setup, and doesn't send their client data to some server they've never heard of.

They are bilingual but default to Spanish in their professional life. Content must meet them there.

### Who We Are Not Talking To

- Teams or agencies (the product is single-agent only)
- Realtors outside Peru (Peru-specific address data, portal templates, and financial tools are core)
- Tech-forward users looking for AI features — the audience values reliability and privacy, not AI for its own sake
- Enterprise buyers evaluating compliance or fleet deployment

## What Makes Bricks Leads Different

These are the genuine differentiators. Use them as angles — not as marketing claims, but as real facts that the audience will recognize.

1. **Todo lo que llega por WhatsApp, organizado automáticamente.** The Share Extension captures a contact from a WhatsApp conversation and extracts the name, phone, property interest, and budget — on the device, no cloud.
2. **Tus datos nunca salen del iPhone.** No server backend. No third-party CRM syncing contact lists. On-device AI. Ley 29733-aware data handling.
3. **Construido para el mercado peruano.** UBIGEO districts, Adondevivir / Urbania / Properati portal templates, alcabala estimator, UIT display, mortgage simulator — built in, offline.
4. **Empareja compradores con propiedades automáticamente.** Deterministic matching by budget, district, bedrooms, area, transaction type. Transparent logic — the agent sees exactly why a match was made.
5. **Nativo Apple. Sin suscripciones de terceros.** One app. No integrations to configure. No monthly fee to a CRM platform.

## Brand Voice — Spanish Application

The Bricks voice translates directly to Spanish: calm, direct, practical, no hype.

### What It Sounds Like

- Directo y claro. Sin exageración.
- Técnicamente honesto — dice lo que hace el producto, no lo que suena bien.
- Útil. Enfocado en resultados reales del trabajo del agente.
- Confiable. Sin urgencia fabricada. Sin presión.

### What It Does Not Sound Like

- Hype: "revolucionario", "el futuro del CRM", "transforma tu negocio", "potencia tus ventas"
- Pressure: "¡No te lo pierdas!", "Oferta limitada", "Únete antes que sea tarde"
- Hollow openers: "¡Emocionado de compartir!", "¡Gran noticia!", "Estamos felices de anunciar"
- Vague value: "Gestión más inteligente", "Experiencia sin fricciones", "El CRM que necesitabas"

### The Voice Formula (in Spanish)

> Di qué hace. Por qué importa. Para.

**Incorrecto:** "¡Estamos emocionados de presentar una nueva forma revolucionaria de gestionar tus clientes en tiempo real!"

**Correcto:** "Bricks Leads captura los datos de un lead de WhatsApp directo a tu CRM. Sin copiar, sin pegar, sin olvidar."

## Content Pillars

Every post belongs to one of these five pillars. Pillar 1 is the primary driver pre-launch.

### 1. El problema que todos conocen
*"Leads lost in WhatsApp" — name the pain so the audience feels seen.*

This is the pre-launch workhorse. No product required. Just name the exact situation a solo Peru realtor lives every week. When they nod along, they're already considering the product.

Angle examples:
- Un cliente te escribe por WhatsApp, respondes, y a los 3 días te olvidaste de hacer seguimiento
- Tienes 15 conversaciones abiertas en WhatsApp y no sabes cuál necesita respuesta hoy
- Tu "CRM" actual es un grupo de WhatsApp contigo mismo
- Buscas el número de un cliente entre 200 mensajes

### 2. Cómo funciona el producto
*Show the product solving the exact problem named in Pillar 1.*

Short, specific, honest. Screen recordings beat descriptions. Show the Share Extension pulling a contact from WhatsApp. Show the matching screen pairing a buyer with a listing. Show the follow-up draft appearing on-device.

Angle examples:
- Capturar un lead de WhatsApp en 3 toques
- Cómo el matching muestra qué propiedades encajan con un comprador
- Redactar un mensaje de seguimiento con IA, revisar, y enviarlo por WhatsApp

### 3. Privacidad y control
*On-device processing, no cloud CRM, data stays on your iPhone.*

This audience may not use the word "privacidad" unprompted, but they will respond to the practical version: "tu lista de clientes no está en ningún servidor."

Angle examples:
- Tu lista de clientes no se sube a ningún servidor
- La IA que redacta tus mensajes funciona en tu iPhone, sin internet
- Qué pasa con los datos de tus clientes cuando usas un CRM en la nube (y qué pasa con Bricks Leads)

### 4. Construido para Perú
*Peru-specific features as proof of genuine commitment to this market.*

This differentiates immediately from generic CRMs. No competitor has UBIGEO search, alcabala calculator, and Properati templates built in and working offline.

Angle examples:
- Búsqueda de distrito por UBIGEO, sin internet
- Plantillas listas para Adondevivir, Urbania y Properati
- Calculadora de alcabala integrada — sin abrir otra app

### 5. Actualizaciones del producto
*What just shipped. Factual, brief, no ceremony.*

Pre-launch: what's being built and why.
Post-launch: what just landed in the update.

Angle examples:
- Lo que estamos construyendo esta semana y por qué decidimos hacerlo así
- Ya disponible: importar leads desde correos de portales
- Se fue un bug que afectaba la búsqueda de distritos en Lima

## Platform Rules

### Instagram

- **Format:** Reels first. Feed posts second. Stories for behind-the-scenes and countdowns.
- **Reels length:** 15–45 seconds. Show, don't describe.
- **Caption length:** 80–150 words. First line must earn the stop-scroll. No hollow openers.
- **Hashtags:** 5–8 targeted. Mix of professional real estate Peru (#inmobiliariaperú, #agenteinmobiliario, #bienesraícesperú) and product (#CRMmóvil, #iPhone, #BricksLeads).
- **What works:** Screen recordings of the Share Extension, matching feature, follow-up drafting. Problem-framing Reels with text overlays. Behind-the-scenes of building for Peru.
- **CTA pre-launch:** "Únete a la lista de espera → leads.bricks.pe" in bio link and caption.
- **CTA post-launch:** "Descarga gratis en el App Store" with direct link.

### X (Twitter)

- **Length:** 280 characters max. One idea per post. No padding.
- **Language:** Spanish. This account speaks to Peru realtors, not to the English-speaking dev community.
- **Tone:** Punchy. Observational. The best Pillar 1 posts read like something the audience would tweet themselves.
- **Hashtags:** 0–2 max. (#InmobiliariaPerú, #BricksLeads only when clearly useful.)
- **Threads:** Only when an idea genuinely needs space — e.g., a teardown of why WhatsApp is failing realtors, or a breakdown of how the matching algorithm works.
- **What works:** Single-sentence problem observations. Short product facts. Honest build updates.
- **CTA:** Link to `leads.bricks.pe` in bio. Pin the waitlist post. Add link to threads only when relevant.

### Facebook and LinkedIn

Currently out of scope for Leads V1. The primary Peru realtor audience is on Instagram and uses WhatsApp — not LinkedIn or Facebook feed. Activate if audience data changes this assumption.

## Pre-Launch Content Cadence

**Phase: Waitlist building. Goal: awareness → email capture at leads.bricks.pe.**

Target: 3–4 posts per week across Instagram + X.

| Day | Platform | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lunes | Instagram | Pillar 1 — El problema | Reel (text overlay, no product) |
| Miércoles | X | Pillar 1 — El problema | Single tweet, observational |
| Jueves | Instagram | Pillar 2 — Cómo funciona | Reel (product demo, short) |
| Viernes | X | Pillar 5 — Build update | Tweet or short thread |

Adjust cadence when something meaningful ships or when a Pillar 1 post gets strong engagement — that's signal to double down on that angle.

### Pre-Launch Content Arc

The narrative should build intentionally over the weeks before launch:

**Weeks 1–2: Name the problem.**
No product. Pure problem content. "¿Cuántos leads has perdido este mes en WhatsApp?" The audience should feel seen before they know the product exists.

**Weeks 3–4: Introduce the solution obliquely.**
"Estamos construyendo algo para esto." Show behind-the-scenes. Let the product appear naturally in context.

**Week 5–6: Show the product directly.**
First demos. Share Extension capture. Matching. The Peru-specific features. Push waitlist CTA clearly.

**Launch week: Switch to acquisition.**
Announce availability. Show the full flow. Drive to App Store. Testimonials or screenshots from early users if available.

## Launch Week Plan

Launch week is a distinct content moment. It replaces the regular cadence.

| Day | Platform | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Día 1 — Lanzamiento | Instagram + X | Anuncio principal. Reel mostrando el flujo completo. CTA directo al App Store. |

## Off-Limits

- Comparaciones negativas con competidores nombrados (CRMs específicos, portales)
- Claims sobre número de usuarios, descargas, o ingresos sin datos verificados
- Anunciar funciones que no han sido lanzadas
- Engagement bait ("Comenta si te pasó", "Etiqueta a un colega que necesita esto")
- Claims legales sobre cumplimiento de Ley 29733 sin revisión legal previa
- Contenido político no relacionado con privacidad o datos
- Cualquier implicación de que el producto envía mensajes automáticamente (no auto-send)
- Claims de IA que impliquen procesamiento en la nube — la IA es on-device

## Measurement

Track these specifically — not vanity metrics.

**Pre-launch:**
- Waitlist signups (primary)
- Landing page sessions from social (UTM-tagged links per platform and pillar)
- Reel plays and completion rate (Instagram)
- Link clicks in bio (Instagram)

**Post-launch:**
- App Store downloads attributable to social (App Store Connect + UTM)
- Activation rate: users who create a first contact or listing within 48 hours
- Upgrade rate: users who hit free tier limit and convert to paid

**UTM convention:**
All links to `leads.bricks.pe` and App Store should use:
`?utm_source=[instagram|x]&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=[pillar-slug]&utm_content=[post-id]`

Example: `leads.bricks.pe?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=el-problema&utm_content=whatsapp-01`
