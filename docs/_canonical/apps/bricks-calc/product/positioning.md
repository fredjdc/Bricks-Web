# Bricks Calc — Positioning

## One-Line Summary

Bricks Calc is a native Apple mortgage calculator that helps users understand the real cost of a home loan with more clarity and less friction.

## Target Audience

- Primary: people evaluating mortgage decisions on Apple devices
- Secondary: real estate professionals and advisors who need to model scenarios with clients

## Core Problem

Most mortgage tools stop at a basic monthly payment. Real decisions require comparing scenarios, modeling prepayments, adding insurance and expenses, and revisiting saved options without losing context.

## Differentiator

Bricks Calc combines a serious mortgage model with native Apple UX, saved scenario management, system integrations, and optional on-device AI guidance. It is not just a generic web calculator wrapped in an app.

## Messaging Pillars

- Clear mortgage analysis, not vague estimates
- Native Apple experience across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and visionOS
- Practical tools for real scenarios: prepayments, expenses, comparisons, reminders, and saved files

## Market Intent & ASO Alignment

Based on active App Store indexing, our positioning strongly aligns with high-intent utility queries. We dominate core terms like **"mortgage calculator"** and **"loan calculator"**, validating our focus on "clear mortgage analysis." Furthermore, broader market reach into terms like **"loan"** and **"tax"** provides opportunities to frame the app as a comprehensive financial tool, ensuring our messaging captures both immediate calculating needs and broader personal finance management.

## What It Is

- A local-first mortgage and housing-cost analysis app
- A decision-support tool for comparing realistic financing scenarios
- An Apple-native experience with widgets, Spotlight, shortcuts, and shareable outputs

## What It Is Not

- Not a loan marketplace
- Not financial advice
- Not a generic calculator with shallow inputs only
- Not dependent on AI to produce the actual financial results

## Guardrails

- Do not position Bricks Calc as a lender, broker, or approval service.
- Do not imply AI-generated numbers or recommendations replace the app's calculation engine.
- Do not reduce the product to "monthly payment only" messaging when the codebase clearly supports deeper scenario analysis.
