## Current Version

**What's New in Bricks Calc**

Welcome to the biggest update to Bricks Calc yet! We've added powerful new features to help you model your real estate financial scenarios better across your Apple devices.

- **Refinance Calculator** — Model a refinance against an existing loan to compute break-even points, monthly savings, and total cost comparison.
- **Rental Income** — Offset your monthly carrying costs with rental income to easily analyze net cash flow for investment properties.
- **Prepayment Engine Rebuild** — We’ve completely rebuilt extra payment scenarios. See how much time and interest you save directly overlaid on your amortization chart.
- **App Onboarding & What's New** — A brand new personalized setup experience with interactive mock calculators, and a streamlined catalog to see what changed in each update.
- **Under the Hood: Core Architectural Refactor** — Major improvements to persistence, workflow, and side-effect logic to make the app faster, more testable, and more reliable.
