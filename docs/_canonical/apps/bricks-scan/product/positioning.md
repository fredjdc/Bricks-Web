# Bricks Scan — Positioning

**Status:** Locked

---

## Positioning Statement

Bricks Scan is a private, on-device document organizer for Apple users who need scanned documents ready, searchable, and clearly organized without manual work.

**Short external version:** Scan documents. Find them fast. Keep everything organized.

---

## Category

Private, on-device document organizer with scanning built in.

---

## Audience

Apple users who handle important documents repeatedly.

---

## Core Problem

Scanning is easy. Keeping documents searchable, named, and organized is not.

---

## Differentiator

Bricks Scan organizes documents on your device automatically, without cloud dependence or manual cleanup.

---

## Functional Value

- Scans become searchable
- Files get clear names
- Documents stay organized
- Everything works on device
- Less manual work after scanning

---

## Emotional Value

- Calm
- Control
- Trust
- Readiness

---

## What Bricks Scan Is

- A document organizer first
- A scanner second
- Private by design
- Built for Apple users
- Focused on what happens after the scan

---

## What Bricks Scan Is Not

- Not just another scanner app
- Not an AI-first hype product
- Not a cloud-first document tool
- Not a bloated office utility

---

## Messaging Pillars

1. Organize
2. Find
3. Private
4. Ready to review, sign, and share
5. Unlimited as premium framing

---

## Positioning Guardrails

**Lead with:**
- scan
- organize
- find
- private
- on your device

**Do not lead with:**
- AI
- feature lists
- signatures
- pricing
- platform count

---

## Internal One-Line Summary

Bricks Scan helps Apple users turn scanned documents into searchable, clearly named, organized files — privately, on their device.

---

## Contrast Claim

Most scanner apps stop at the scan. Bricks Scan keeps going.
