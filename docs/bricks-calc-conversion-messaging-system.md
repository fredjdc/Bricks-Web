# Bricks Calc — Conversion Messaging System

**Status:** V1
**Purpose:** Keep Bricks Calc messaging aligned across App Store, onboarding, in-app Store, website, Apple Ads, and future Custom Product Pages.
**Primary goal:** Improve conversion by moving users through the funnel with one consistent promise.

---

## 1. Strategic Summary

Bricks Calc should not be positioned as only a mortgage calculator.

The strongest acquisition entry point is still simple:

> **Estimate your monthly payment.**

But the stronger product promise is broader:

> **Start with the payment. Then plan what changes over time.**

This creates the right hierarchy:

1. **Fast estimate** — immediate value.
2. **Real monthly cost** — stronger differentiation.
3. **Planning tools** — compare, prepay, refinance, track.
4. **Long-term utility** — save, update, and manage scenarios over time.

The funnel should not lead with complexity. It should lead with the user's immediate search intent, then reveal the deeper product value after the first result.

---

## 2. Core Positioning

### Primary Positioning

**Bricks Calc helps homebuyers estimate the real monthly cost of a mortgage, then compare, adjust, and track what changes over time.**

### Short Positioning

**Estimate the payment. Plan what changes.**

### Expanded Positioning

**Bricks Calc is a mortgage planning companion for before, during, and after the loan. Estimate quickly, compare scenarios, test extra payments, check refinance options, and keep mortgage details organized across devices.**

### Strategic Rule

Use the full “before, during, and after” story as the product vision, not as the first-use promise.

The first-use promise should stay focused:

> **Get the first estimate fast.**

## 5. Messaging Principles

### Do

* Lead with the payment.
* Use plain mortgage language.
* Show the next useful action.
* Keep advanced features secondary until the user has seen a result.
* Tie every message to a funnel step.
* Be clear about what is free and what is unlocked.

### Do Not

* Lead with every feature at once.
* Make the app sound like generic finance software.
* Overpromise property management if the feature is not central yet.
* Use vague words like “powerful,” “seamless,” or “all-in-one.”
* Ask for precision before showing value.
* Make “free” the main promise.

## 8. In-App Store / Paywall Messaging System

### Product Access Model

Free users can:

* Run mortgage calculations.
* Use Cloud Sync.
* Save up to 5 calculations.
* Run extra payment scenarios.
* Save up to 3 extra payment plans.
* Use comparison.
* Use rental income.
* Use refinance.
* Use payment reminders.
* Add expenses without limits.

Paid unlocks:

* Unlimited saved calculations.
* Unlimited saved extra payment plans.

Products:

* Yearly Access.
* One-Time Payment.

### Paywall Role

The paywall should not imply that all planning tools are locked.

It should frame the purchase around continuity:

> **Keep every plan, scenario, and extra payment saved.**

### Recommended Paywall Title

**Save every mortgage plan**

### Recommended Paywall Description

**Keep unlimited calculations and extra payment plans saved across iPhone, iPad, and Mac.**

### Recommended Benefit List

* Unlimited saved calculations
* Unlimited saved extra payment plans
* Side-by-side comparisons
* Sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac

### Trust Copy

**Cancel anytime. Secured by the App Store.**

### Independent Builder Copy

**Built and maintained independently. Your purchase helps keep Bricks Calc focused, private, and improving.**

### Pricing Card Copy

#### One-Time Payment

**One-Time Payment**
Save calculations and extra payment plans without limits.

#### Yearly Access

**Yearly Access**
Unlimited saves for one year.

### Avoid

* “Plan before, during, and after the loan” as the paywall headline.
* Listing features as paid if they are free.
* Making the user think refinance, comparison, rental income, reminders, or expenses are locked.

## 15. Final Operating Rule

Every front-end message should answer one of these questions:

1. **Can I estimate the payment quickly?**
2. **Can I understand the real cost?**
3. **Can I compare my options?**
4. **Can I keep my mortgage plan updated over time?**
5. **Why should I unlock unlimited saves?**

If a message does not answer one of these, remove it or move it lower in the experience.
