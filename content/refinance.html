Before you refinance.

Is Bricks Calc free?

Yes. Save up to three plans and sync with iCloud for free. Pro adds Shared Costs and unlimited saved plans, extra payments, and expenses.

Can I compare my current loan with a refinance?

Yes. Compare up to three plans, including your current loan and new fixed-rate offers. Review monthly payments, remaining versus new-loan interest, and terms. Results are estimates; confirm loan details with your lender.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No Bricks account is required, and calculations work offline. iCloud sync is free and uses your iCloud account and an internet connection to sync saved plans across your Apple devices.

How should I weigh refinance closing costs?

Ask your lender for closing costs. For a simple break-even estimate, divide those costs by any monthly payment savings. Consider how long you will keep the loan and whether the new term adds years or interest.

Will extra payments lower my required payment?

Usually not. Extra principal can reduce interest and shorten the loan, but a lower required payment generally needs a recast or refinance. Confirm your options and any fees with your lender.

Which devices are supported?

Bricks Calc is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.
