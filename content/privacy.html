Privacy policy

Last updated: April 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bricks Apps. This privacy policy applies to our apps and games, and explains how we handle your information when you use our applications.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect your personal data or inputs from our apps and games. All data and calculations are processed locally on your device. However, we may collect limited diagnostic information and aggregated usage data to improve app performance and reliability. We do not use advertising services or third-party advertising SDKs.

a. Information you provide

App data: information, inputs, and data you enter into our apps and games for calculations, gameplay, or other features. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

b. Diagnostics and usage data (optional)

Optional diagnostics and analytics: crash reports, performance metrics, device type and OS version, and aggregated feature usage to help us improve the apps. Where available, you can control analytics collection in your device settings.

c. Information we do not collect

We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number.
We do not request or store financial account details.
We do not track your location.

3. How we use your information

Local storage: all your data is stored locally on your device.
App functionality: we use the information you provide solely to enable app features and functionality on your device.
Export and sharing: when you choose to export or share content from our apps, we temporarily process your data on your device to generate PDFs, images, or other files for sharing.
App improvements: we may use anonymized diagnostics and aggregated usage metrics to improve features, performance, and reliability.

4. Data sharing and third parties

No external data transfer: your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.
Sharing features: when you use the app’s sharing features to export or share content, you control who receives this information.
Apple’s App Store: we use Apple for managing in-app purchases, which is subject to Apple’s privacy practices.

5. Data retention and security

Local storage only: all data is stored locally on your device.
Data control: you can delete your saved data at any time through the app interface.

6. Your rights and choices

Delete data: you can delete any or all saved data within the app.
Reset settings: you can reset all app settings to defaults.
Export control: you control when and how your app data is exported or shared.

7. Premium features

Our apps and games offer premium features through in-app purchases, which are processed by Apple. These purchases only affect feature availability and do not change our data practices.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes through our apps.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hello@bricks.pe.
