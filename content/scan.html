FAQ

Common questions.

Is Bricks Scan only for Apple devices?

Yes. Bricks Scan is built natively for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It uses Apple's built-in OCR and integrates with Spotlight, Calendar, and Reminders.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Scanning, search, organization, and signing all work completely offline. An internet connection is never required.

Where are my documents stored?

On your device. Bricks Scan never uploads your documents to any server. iCloud Sync is available but optional — you choose when to enable it.

Can I search inside document text?

Yes. Every scan is run through OCR, making the full text searchable. You can find any document by the words it contains, not just its name.

Is it free?

Yes. You can download and start using Bricks Scan for free from the App Store. No account required to get started.

Does it sync across my devices?

Yes, optionally. Enable iCloud Sync in settings to access your scans across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Off by default — your data stays local until you choose otherwise.
