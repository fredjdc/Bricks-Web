FAQ

Questions about the beta.

Is Bricks Leads only for Apple devices?
Yes. Bricks Leads runs natively on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. The on-device AI features require an Apple Intelligence-capable device.
Where is my data stored?
On your device. Leads is local-first — nothing leaves your iPhone. A future update will add optional iCloud sync across your own Apple devices. No server backend, no team syncing.
Does it work in Spanish and for Peru?
Yes. Leads supports Spanish, Peru addresses (distrito, UBIGEO), portal templates for Adondevivir, Urbania, and Properati, and Peru financial tools including alcabala, UIT, and mortgage and commission calculators.
Does the AI send messages for me?
No. Apple Intelligence drafts suggested messages on your device. You review and edit every one, then send through iMessage, WhatsApp, or Mail yourself. Nothing goes out automatically.
When does it launch publicly?
We’re aiming for Q3 2026. The beta runs until then.
Does beta access expire?
No. Your beta access stays active until the public launch. You won’t get kicked out mid-deal.
Will my data carry over at launch?
Yes. Every lead, property, and follow-up you add in the beta transfers to the launch version. Nothing resets.
How much does it cost?
Free during the beta. And every beta tester gets a free 2-year subscription after launch — our thank-you for helping shape it.
Who should join the beta?
Solo realtors in Peru who use Apple devices and want a CRM that stays out of the way. If that sounds like you, request access below.
