Bricks Calc
Simulator

The simulator guides data entry through three sequential steps. Complete each step to unlock the next.

Step 1 — Property

Currency

Select the currency using the picker in the top bar. The default currency is configured in Settings.

Home price

Enter the total property price. Calculations update automatically as you type.

Down payment

Enter the down payment amount or select a quick percentage: 10%, 12.5%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%. The loan amount is automatically calculated as Home Price minus Down Payment.

Step 2 — Loan terms

Interest rate

Enter the annual interest rate (%) your lender is offering.

Loan term

Enter the term in years or months using the type picker next to the field. Quick year options: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. For exact terms, switch to months and type the number directly.

Disbursement date

Set the exact date when the loan will be disbursed. This determines the dates in the amortization schedule.

Step 3 — Taxes & insurance (optional)

Enable the "Include taxes and insurance" toggle to add these costs to the monthly payment. Each field accepts an annual percentage or a fixed monthly amount.

Life insurance

Annual percentage of the loan balance, or a fixed monthly amount.

Home insurance

Annual percentage of the home value, or a fixed monthly amount.

Property tax

Annual percentage of the home value, or a fixed monthly amount.

Tips

  • The calculator automatically validates fields before calculating.
  • Required fields must be completed before advancing to the next step.
  • Results can be saved for later comparison or reference from Files.
  • The default currency and interest rate are configured in Settings.
Results

The results screen shows a complete summary of your mortgage and provides access to detailed analysis tools.

Main metrics

Monthly payment

Shows the total monthly payment with an optional breakdown of principal and interest, insurance, and taxes.

Loan details

Total interest, loan amount, down payment, home price, and term.

Charts

Loan progress

Pie chart showing the percentage of principal paid to date.

Monthly expenses breakdown

Visualization of the monthly payment composition: principal and interest, insurance, and taxes.

Rental income

Tap "Add rental income" on the results screen to record rental income and see the impact on your net monthly obligation.

Monthly income

Estimated monthly rental amount.

Vacancy rate

Percentage of time without a tenant. The app automatically calculates the net effective income.

Amortization schedule

Access the full payment schedule from the results screen. It shows principal, interest, and remaining balance for each installment. Use the "Jump to current" button to scroll to the active installment.

Actions

Edit

Returns to the simulator to modify the inputs.

Save

Saves the calculation with a name for future access from Files.

Share

Generates and shares the result as an image.

Prepayment calculator

The Prepayment Calculator lets you model one-time or recurring extra payments and instantly see how they reduce your interest and loan term.

Setting up a prepayment

Starting installment

Use the slider to choose which installment the prepayment begins on. The installment number and its corresponding month are shown above the slider.

Amount

The extra amount applied each time. Tap the field to enter a value.

Repeat

Choose Once for a single extra payment, or Monthly to apply the extra amount every installment from the starting point.

Ends

Choose when the recurring prepayment stops:

Until payoff

The extra payment continues every period until the loan is fully paid off.

Installment

The extra payment stops at a specific installment. Use the slider below the toggle to set the ending installment.

Type

Choose how the extra payment is applied:

Reduce term

The extra amount reduces the number of remaining installments. Your periodic payment stays the same, but you pay off the loan earlier.

Reduce payment

The extra amount lowers your ongoing periodic payment. The loan term stays the same, but each installment costs less.

Projected impact

As you adjust the settings, the Projected Impact section updates in real time. It shows:

Savings

Total interest you avoid paying over the life of the loan.

Interests

A before-and-after comparison of total interest paid — original versus with the prepayment applied.

Tips

  • Earlier prepayments save more interest — the sooner principal is reduced, the less interest accrues.
  • Use Until Payoff with a monthly repeat to model a consistent extra payment strategy.
  • Try Reduce Term vs Reduce Payment to compare strategies with the same extra amount.
  • Tap the share icon at the top to save or send your prepayment scenario.
Expenses

The Expenses tab, inside Financials on the results screen, lets you record additional property-related costs and see their impact on the total monthly cost.

Available frequencies

One-time

A cost that occurs only once (e.g. closing costs, moving expenses).

Monthly

A cost that repeats every month (e.g. maintenance, HOA fees).

Semester

A cost that occurs twice a year.

Annual

A cost that occurs once a year (e.g. insurance renewal).

Tips

  • Expenses are automatically grouped by frequency for easy review.
  • Monthly, semester, and annual expenses are normalized to a monthly equivalent in the total cost summary.
  • When you have 12 or more expenses, a search bar appears to filter them quickly.
Files

Files is your centralized panel for managing all your saved mortgage calculations. From here you can view, compare, and organize your scenarios in one place.

View & organize

Tap to open

Tap any calculation to open its results.

Row details

Each row shows the name, monthly payment, next payment date, and a principal progress bar.

iCloud sync

Calculations are automatically synced via iCloud across your devices.

Swipe actions

Swipe left on a calculation to reveal quick actions.

Duplicate

Creates a copy of the calculation with the same base values.

More

Opens a menu with options to Rename or Delete the calculation.

Selection mode

Tap the "Select" button in the top bar to enable multi-selection mode.

Compare

Select 2 or 3 calculations and tap "Compare" to see a side-by-side comparison with key metrics for each.

Bulk delete

Select two or more calculations and tap the trash icon to delete them all at once.

Bricks Scan
Scan

Bricks Scan captures physical documents with your device camera and converts them into searchable digital files. All processing happens on-device — no document content is sent to external servers.

Capture methods

Camera

Tap the "Scan" button to open the camera. The app detects edges and corrects perspective automatically. Capture multiple pages in one session.

Import from Photos

Select one or more images from your photo library. They go through the same processing pipeline as camera scans.

Import PDF

Import existing PDFs from the Files app or iCloud Drive. Multi-page PDFs become a single document with all their pages.

Automatic processing

Image enhancement

Brightness and contrast adjustment, and auto-crop (configurable in Settings → Quality).

OCR

On-device text extraction using Apple’s Vision framework.

Auto-organization

Suggestion or assignment of folders and tags based on content (configurable in Settings → Preferences).

Tips

  • Scan in a well-lit environment for best OCR results.
  • You can add more pages to an existing document from its detail view.
Documents

Each saved document contains the scanned images, OCR-extracted text, metadata, and notes. From the detail view you can read, edit, and manage all pages.

OCR text & intelligence

Extracted text

OCR-recognized text is shown in each page’s view. You can search within the document and found text is visually highlighted.

Automatic title and summary

With Apple Intelligence available, the app automatically generates a descriptive title and a content summary. Both are editable.

Extracted metadata

The app extracts key fields from the content: total amount, vendor, date, address, and more. All editable in the metadata panel.

Page management

Reorder and delete

Open the Page Organizer to drag pages or delete ones you don’t need.

Rotate and crop

Non-destructive operations — the original image is preserved and changes can be reverted.

Notes

Add notes at the document level or per page. Notes are included in full-text search.

Smart lists & search

All Documents

Shows all saved documents regardless of folder.

Past Week

Documents created or modified in the last 7 days.

Trash

Deleted documents. Permanently removed after 30 days.

Tips

  • Search finds matches across the title, OCR text, notes, tags, and metadata. It also works from system Spotlight.
  • Pin your most important documents by tapping "Pin" to keep them at the top of the list.
  • The free tier supports up to 12 documents. Premium unlocks unlimited storage.
Organize

Bricks Scan organizes your documents using Folders and Tags. You can assign them manually or let the app do it automatically based on content.

Folders

Custom folders

Create custom folders from the Organization view. A document can belong to multiple folders simultaneously.

Limits

The free tier supports up to 20 folders. Premium unlocks unlimited folders.

Tags

Default tags

Invoice, Receipt, Contract, ID & Passport, Medical, Others. Create custom tags with your own name, color, and icon.

Limits

The free tier supports up to 12 tags. Premium unlocks unlimited tags.

Auto-organization

Configure the behavior in Settings → Preferences:

Off

Does not suggest or auto-assign folders or tags.

Suggest only

Shows suggestions you can accept or dismiss.

Auto

Assigns folders and tags directly without confirmation. Accuracy is tuned with the "Strictness" option.

Annotate & sign

Markup

Drawing tools

Tap "Markup" on a page view to open the markup editor, powered by Apple’s PencilKit. Supports Apple Pencil on iPad and finger input on iPhone and iPad.

Non-destructive

Annotations are stored non-destructively and can be included or excluded when exporting.

Signatures

Saved signatures

Draw your signature once and save it. You can apply it to any page with a single tap. Signatures are stored non-destructively.

Limits

The free tier allows saving up to 2 signatures. Premium unlocks unlimited signatures.

Export & share

Tap "Export" in the document detail view to open the export panel. Choose the format, options, and share directly from the app.

Formats

PDF

"Embed OCR in PDF" makes the PDF searchable with Cmd+F or Ctrl+F. "Protect with password" encrypts the PDF before sharing.

JPEG, Text & HTML

Export pages as JPEG images, OCR text as a .txt file, or the full document with images as HTML. Quality is configured in Settings → Quality.

Export options

Include markup

Toggle to include or exclude PencilKit annotations in the exported file.

Include signatures

Toggle to include or exclude signatures placed on pages.

Scope

Choose to export the current page or the full document.

Tips

  • After generating the file, the native share sheet lets you send it via AirDrop, Mail, Messages, save to iCloud Drive, or open in any compatible app.
  • Text export is useful for copying content from physical documents directly to other applications.
