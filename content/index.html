Before you get started.

Is Bricks Calc free?

Yes. You can download it for free and save up to three plans. An optional upgrade removes limits and gives you more flexibility.

Can I compare different mortgage offers?

Yes. Compare up to three plans to see how different home prices, down payments, and interest rates affect your monthly payment and total cost.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. The app works offline, and you can use iCloud to sync across your Apple devices.

Can I use Bricks Calc if I already own a home?

Yes. Explore extra payments, refinancing options, amortization schedules, and changes to your current mortgage.

Are the calculations exact?

Bricks Calc provides planning estimates. Your lender can confirm the actual loan terms, fees, and final payment amounts.

Which devices are supported?

Bricks Calc is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.
