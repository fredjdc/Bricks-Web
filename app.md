## Public description

Plan your mortgage with clear numbers.

Bricks Calc is a mortgage calculator and loan calculator for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. Calculate monthly payments, compare loan scenarios, and understand the full cost before you commit.

Adjust the loan amount, down payment, interest rate, and term. Add taxes, insurance, PMI, rental income, expenses, or extra payments. See the impact instantly.

No spreadsheets. No account required. No ads.

### What you can do

Calculate monthly payments  
Estimate mortgage and loan payments with rate, term, down payment, taxes, insurance, and PMI.

See the full monthly cost  
Add expenses, insurance, fees, and rental income to understand the real cost of the loan.

Save and compare plans  
Keep different rates, terms, expenses, and payment plans side by side.

Understand the loan over time  
View the amortization schedule and see how principal and interest change month by month.

Test extra payments  
Add one-time or recurring extra payments. See interest saved and time saved.

Compare refinance options  
Compare your current loan with a new option and see when refinancing starts to make sense.

### Built for Apple devices

Private by design  
Your calculations stay on your device.

No account required  
Start calculating without creating a profile.

iCloud sync  
Keep saved plans available across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.

Native on Apple devices  
Built for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.

### Pricing

Bricks Calc is free to try.

Unlock unlimited saved plans, extra payment plans, expense plans, and split payments with an in-app purchase. A one-time payment option is available.

The repository's current canonical ASO document records this 100-character field:

`loan,app,tax,rental,pmi,debt,home,real,estate,house,equity,rate,property,buy,free,easy,escrow,fha,va`

Do not target HELOC, rent-vs-buy, closing-cost, FHA, VA, USDA, or live-rate calculator pages until the product clearly supports the full search intent. Traffic that finds a mismatched feature does not convert.
